    Frequently Asked Questions

    Click on "Access Logbook" or "New Log Entry" in Quick Actions. Fill out the weekly template with your activities, learning outcomes, and reflections. Submit for supervisor review.

    You can still upload entries for previous weeks. However, it's important to maintain regular weekly submissions. Contact your supervisor if you need assistance catching up.

    Go to "Submit Assignment" in Quick Actions or navigate to the Assignments section. Upload your files, add any notes, and submit. You'll receive feedback from your supervisor.

    Once you've completed all 8 weeks of logbook entries, click "Generate Report" in Quick Actions. The system will automatically compile your entries into a professional report.

    For technical issues, contact your supervisor first. For urgent technical problems, reach out during support hours (Mon-Fri, 9AM-5PM) or check with your institution's IT department.
